# Simulated Concept Test: What Gets People to Check MGP Before a Bet

**Date:** 2026-11-21 · **Asked by:** owner (is an "MGP Score" worth it?) · **Raw data:** `2026-11-21-game-page-concepts-data/`

**Simulated, not real.** Same method and caveats as `2026-11-20-simulated-survey.md`: four AI-generated panels of 250 (online betting communities, general US bettors, industry professionals, newer bettors in newly legal states). Use it to narrow options, then confirm with real users.

## Concepts tested (on a game page)

| Code | Concept |
|---|---|
| K | **Start Here**: the 3 to 5 most notable facts about the game, ranked, in plain English ("Oregon has won 4 straight.") |
| S | **MGP Score**: a 0 to 100 score for each side, combining form, matchup, against-the-spread history, rest, injuries and line movement |
| V | **Side-by-Side**: bars comparing both teams on form, offense, defense, ATS, rest, injuries |
| A | **Ask The Analyst**: ask a question about the game |

## Results

**Which one would make you open MGP before a bet?**

| | Share | Panels A, B, C, D |
|---|---|---|
| Start Here | **25.1%** | 56, 58, 70, 67 |
| MGP Score | 22.6% | 46, 60, 66, 54 |
| None of these | 21.5% | 64, 65, 33, 53 |
| Ask The Analyst | 15.5% | 45, 28, 45, 37 |
| Side-by-Side | 15.3% | 39, 39, 36, 39 |

**Usefulness (1 to 5):** Start Here 3.46 (highest in every panel) · Side-by-Side 3.21 · Ask The Analyst 3.10 · MGP Score 3.09.

**Would you trust an MGP Score?** Trust it 15.6% · only if shown how it's calculated 39.2% · don't trust 19.7% · "it's just a pick in disguise" 25.5%.

**Would an MGP Score change your trust in the app?** More 27.7% · same 41.5% · **less 30.8%**. It lowers trust more than it raises it overall, and much more with online communities (89 less vs 53 more) and industry (105 vs 72).

**Industry risk flags (compliance, App Store, responsible gaming), of 250:** MGP Score **71** · Ask The Analyst 36 · Start Here 9 · Side-by-Side 6.

**What people check before betting a game (any that apply):** injuries 78.5% · recent form and streaks 67.6% · team stats 53.6% · expert opinions 45.2% · player props 44.1% · line movement 43.8% · head-to-head 36.5% · against-the-spread record 35.9% · public betting 33.2% · weather 28.2% · rest and travel 24.1%.

**How many places people check before a bet:** one 16.1% · two to three 44.0% · four to six 29.2% · seven or more 10.7%. 84% check two or more places; 40% check four or more.

## Findings

1. **Start Here is the hook.** It won "would open before a bet" and usefulness in every panel, with almost no risk flags. Plain English beats jargon: casual bettors understand "won 4 straight" but not "against the spread".
2. **The MGP Score pulls in people who want a pick, and pushes away people who'd trust MGP.** Its fans are mostly casual bettors who read it as a lean ("if it says Oregon 78, I'm tailing the 78"). Serious bettors, industry people and compliance see a pick in disguise. It drew more risk flags than the other three concepts combined, and it would be judged on wins and losses ("if it goes 3-9, they'll roast me").
3. **If a score ever ships, it has to show its work.** "Trust it only if shown how it's calculated" was the biggest answer (39%).
4. **"One place" is a real need.** Most bettors check two or more sources; four in ten check four or more.
5. **About one in five won't open any of it.** Gut bettors and sharps with bigger odds screens. Don't build for them first.
6. **Order the game page by what people check:** injuries, form, team stats, then odds and line movement, props, head-to-head and against-the-spread.
7. **The Analyst needs to show its sources.** People doubt AI accuracy, and compliance people doubt the "no picks" guardrail holds when users push. Every answer should say where its numbers come from and when.
