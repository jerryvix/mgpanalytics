# Simulated Survey: Headlines and What Leads Home

**Date:** 2026-11-20 · **Asked by:** owner (review board note 4) · **Raw data:** `2026-11-20-survey-data/` (four panel files, `aggregate.py`, `aggregate-output.txt`)

## Read this first: what this is and isn't

This is a **simulated** survey. 1,000 respondents were generated by four independent AI runs, each playing a differently recruited panel of 250. It is useful for ruling options out and spotting risks, not as proof of what real bettors want. All four panels come from the same kind of model, so their agreement shows consistency, not truth. Validate the winner with real people before spending money on it (see "How to check with real people").

## Findings

1. **"Beat the books" style promises hurt more than they help.** B, H and C won some casual bettors but serious bettors, DFS players and industry people read them as tout language ("another Telegram capper"). B was flagged as a compliance or App Review risk by 149 of 250 industry respondents. Guaranteed-win claims are the number one reason people distrust a betting app (65%).
2. **The owner's framings tested last.** "The upper hand the pros have" (C) and "Level the playing field" (D) were the two least chosen headlines in every panel. The idea behind them (pro-level information for everyone) is carried better by concrete headlines like G and F.
3. **Concrete beats promising.** F won 3 of 4 panels because it says what you see, not what you'll win. But F claims something MGP can't show: line moves are not money flow. MGP has no handle or ticket data, and `docs/positioning.md` lists "sharp money indicators" under DO NOT CLAIM. Traders in panel C called it misleading, and some casual bettors read it as a budgeting app.
4. **E is the steadiest.** "Bet smarter, not harder." never ranked below 4th, had the second-fewest compliance flags, and was the casual bettors' favorite in two panels.
5. **People want their own teams first.** Your teams beat line moves about 2 to 1 for general and newer bettors. Line moves won only with online-community and industry panels.
6. **Guest browsing is right.** Only 5.8% would create an account before seeing anything; 40% distrust apps that force it (D-014).
7. **Stale odds are a credibility risk on par with bad copy.** In the industry panel, stale or wrong odds (188) beat guaranteed-win claims (179) as a delete reason. Every number needs an "as of" time.
8. **The two most-wanted paid features are props tools and faster line-move alerts.** Faster alerts are also MGP's main data cost, so a future Pro tier could pay for its own faster odds refresh.
9. **Explain the Analyst by what it does.** Casual bettors asked "an AI that won't tell me what to bet, then what's it for?" Lead with what it does ("Ask anything about tonight's games"). Serious bettors trusted it more because it doesn't give picks.
10. **"Insiders" can read as inside information.** Add a one-line description under the tab title ("Well-known betting voices, in one place") so nobody thinks MGP sells inside information.
11. **Notification spam is the second-biggest delete reason (51%).** Cap pushes per day and keep every alert type opt-in.

## Recommendations

| Where | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Main headline (store listing, welcome screen) | **"Bet smarter, not harder."** Steadiest across panels, low compliance risk, already owner-approved for /why |
| Supporting line | "Every number you need. One app." Closest to the internal north star, lowest compliance risk |
| Line-move module title | "See where the lines are moving." Keeps F's concrete appeal without the money-flow claim (this wording itself was not tested) |
| Avoid in store copy | "Beat the sportsbooks", "Bet like a pro", "The upper hand the pros have", and "Get an edge on the books" (110 of 250 industry flags; fine on the website if the owner keeps it, risky in an App Store listing) |
| Home order | Your teams first when you've picked teams; biggest line moves first for guests who haven't. Streaks lower down, with sample sizes ("12 of last 15") |
| Pro tier (v1.1) | Props tools and faster line-move alerts |

## How to check with real people (no added cost)

- **App Store product page optimization** (free, once live): test up to three versions of the screenshots and their headlines against real store visitors.
- **Custom product pages** (free): send different links to different audiences and compare.
- **One poll** on MGP's own X or Reddit presence before launch, with the top three headlines.
