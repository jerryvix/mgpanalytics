# Packaging public betting data: Today, Pulse and the game page

**Date:** 2026-11-23 · **Status:** proposal, pending owner review (DECISIONS D-055) · **Mockups:** canvas page "Splits First · Pulse" (https://claude.ai/artifact/7ZWWFemMUESwBoLiFsEk3i), built from real data

## The job

One place, every game, every sport: where the bets are, where the money is, what the trends say, and how it turned out. Today this lives in scattered posts from sportsbook PR accounts and analysts on X. They cover a handful of NFL games once a week, each in a different format, and none of it can be filtered by sport, searched or followed.

## What the owner's examples contain

The Nov 23 screenshots (an analyst's posts and a sportsbook's weekly "most bet" post) hold three kinds of facts:

1. **Market facts:** share of bets and money on a side ("79% of money on Chiefs −15.5"), and lists built from them: most bet teams by tickets and by money, underdog moneylines, totals, player props, anytime TD scorers.
2. **Trend facts:** a record in a situation ("NFL favorites are 30-13-1 ATS since Week 8"; "Mahomes is 5-0 ATS in his last 5 as a double-digit favorite"), often with a contrast ("10-0 SU but 2-8 ATS").
3. **Receipts:** the result afterward (a final score card, a check or a cross).

The strongest posts combine all three: a trend, where the money is, then the result. MGP can produce all three automatically for every game.

## The package

### Today

Every row: matchup, time, line, total, and one plain sentence about the market: "78% of money · 85% of bets on CIN". When money and bets point different ways, a green **Money vs Bets** tag ("93% of money on CHA +3.5 · 62% of bets on MEM"). The beating pulse from the MGP mark now means this. One source line at the bottom: "Bets and money from DraftKings · Updated 3:05 PM".

### Pulse (new tab, replaces Insiders)

Insiders depended on the X API, which is on hold for cost (D-023). Pulse produces the same kind of content from our own data, for every game, in one format. Three views, one sport at a time:

- **Bets & Money:** leaderboards for the week, the live version of the sportsbook's weekly post: Most Money, Most Bets, Money vs Bets, Underdogs Getting Money, Most Bet Totals. Each row opens the game.
- **Trends:** generated facts for this week's games and the league, strongest first.
- **Results:** the receipts: how the side with the most money did this week and this season, game by game ("90% of money on Patriots −7 · Patriots 24, Dolphins 19 · No Cover").

### Game page

Order: **Bets & Money** (spread, total and moneyline, each with a bets bar and a money bar), a Money vs Bets sentence when they disagree, **Trends** (the top 3 to 5 for this game), What to Know, Odds. Opening and current lines stay in Odds as a detail (D-053).

### Alerts (if time allows in v1)

Money alerts on games you follow: the money side flips, or one side passes 70% of the money.

### The Analyst

Answers from the same stored facts ("Where's the money on Thanksgiving?"), so its numbers always match the screens.

## How facts are written

- One fact per line. Subject first, then the record: "Cowboys are 5-11 ATS on Thanksgiving since 2010".
- Always the full record (W-L, or W-L-P with pushes) and the window (this season, since Week 8, since 2024). Never a bare percentage for a record.
- Lines in sportsbook notation through `packages/shared/odds/format.ts` (D-039). Percentages as whole numbers.
- Describe, never advise: no "should", "lock", "fade" or claims about who is sharp (CLAUDE.md, `docs/positioning.md`). Results read "Covered", "No Cover" or "Push"; no emoji.
- Sample floors: a situational trend needs at least 8 games unless it is phrased "last N"; a league-wide trend needs at least 30.

## Trend engine (backend)

- A nightly job per sport evaluates a template library against results and closing lines for every upcoming game and stores rows: game, template, subject, record, window, sample size, strength, text.
- **Templates v1:** team ATS (season, home and away, favorite and underdog, spread buckets including 10+ points), team over/under, QB ATS and SU as favorite and underdog (NFL), spots (Thanksgiving, Monday and Thursday night, NBA back-to-backs), league-wide (favorites, home teams, overs, "since Week N"), and money and bets results (sides with 70%+).
- **Ranking:** strength is how far a record sits from 50% given its sample (Wilson lower bound); a straight-up versus ATS contrast gets a boost; show 3 to 5 per game.
- **Text comes from templates, not the AI,** so no number is ever invented. The Analyst quotes stored facts.
- **Receipts job:** after finals, mark the money side and the bets side Covered, No Cover or Push from the last pre-kickoff capture, and update the season records.
- **Cost:** none beyond Supabase. nflverse and CollegeFootballData are free; check their terms before launch.

## Edge cases

- **Early numbers:** days before kickoff many games read 100% / 0% because almost nobody has bet yet, and DraftKings gives no volume. Hold a game out of leaderboards and show "Too Early" until both sides are above 0% or kickoff is near (starting rule: 24 hours for NFL, game day for basketball).
- **One book:** the numbers reflect DraftKings customers only. Say so once per screen in the source line.
- **Line changes:** money accumulates across several numbers during the week; always show the current line beside the percentage.
- **Closing read:** receipts use the last capture before kickoff (the sync already stops at kickoff).
- **Pushes, pick'em lines, games taken off the board:** show no split rather than a stale one.
- **Rescheduled and neutral-site games:** match on teams and date window as the sync already does.
- **The source changes again:** it did on Nov 2 and nobody noticed for three weeks. The parser now fails loudly; add a freshness check to the ops health report (`scripts/ops/health-check.mjs`) so a stale split shows up within hours.
- **Line sources disagree by a game here and there:** expected; we state our source.
- **Licensing:** scraping a public page and using open datasets; review before any paywall (`APP_STORE_COMPLIANCE.md`).
